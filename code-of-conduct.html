Our community

Respectful, welcoming, focused.

Everyone at Café des Langues — students, teachers, and staff — is expected to keep classes and school spaces respectful, welcoming, and focused. By enrolling in a class, you agree to this code of conduct.

✓ Expected behaviour

  • Treat all students, teachers, and staff with respect, regardless of accent, ability, nationality, background, or level.
  • Be patient with others' pace of learning — mistakes are part of the process.
  • Join sessions on time, with your camera on where expected, and mute your mic when not speaking.
  • Do not record, screenshot, or share any part of a live class without permission from the teacher and other participants.
  • Keep school chats and forums on-topic — no unrelated advertising or spam.

✕ Unacceptable behaviour

  • Harassment, bullying, or discriminatory remarks of any kind, in English or French.
  • Persistent disruption of classes after being asked to stop.
  • Sharing or recording class content without consent.
  • Impersonating a teacher, staff member, or student.

Reporting a concern

Tell us what happened.

If you experience or witness behaviour that goes against this code of conduct, contact us with as much detail as possible. Reports are handled confidentially, and we do not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.

Email cafedeslangues.edu@gmail.com

Consequences

How we respond.

Depending on severity, Café des Langues may issue a warning, temporarily suspend access to classes, or permanently remove a student or teacher from the school without refund.

Changes to this document

Café des Langues may update these terms from time to time. Continued enrollment after an update means you accept the revised version. This page covers our code of conduct in full — see La Carte for pricing, payment, and attendance terms.
