What we're about

A café table, not a classroom.

Café des Langues teaches French the way it's actually used — in conversation, at a comfortable pace, with people who notice how you learn. Every class happens live online, so you get real feedback in the moment, not just a video to watch alone.

Whether you're starting from zero or polishing fluency for work, travel, or family, there's a format built for you.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need to attend classes live?

Yes — all lessons, whether group or one-on-one, are delivered live online. You'll also get videos, slides, and notes to reinforce what you learn between sessions.

What if I can't make a one-on-one session?

Notify your teacher at least 12 hours before the scheduled time and you won't be charged for that session. If you miss a session without notice, a ₦5,000 penalty applies.

How does payment work for one-on-one sessions?

It's billed hourly at ₦15,000, split into two payments: 50% upfront, and the remaining 50% before the last week of the month. If your monthly hours pass 10, a 15% discount applies.

How does payment work for group classes?

Group classes are billed at a flat ₦50,000 per month.

How do I report a concern about someone's behaviour?

Email cafedeslangues.edu@gmail.com with as much detail as you can. Reports are handled confidentially, and we don't retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.

How do I enroll?

Head to our enrollment page — it walks through choosing a format, filling in the form, and getting your first class scheduled.
