Enroll

Pull up a chair.

Enrolling takes a few minutes. Here's how it works, and what happens after you submit the form.

Take the placement assessment

Optional, but it takes about 2 minutes and helps us (and you) know where to start. Scroll down to try it now.

Pick your format

Group class or one-on-one — see La Carte if you're still deciding.

Fill in the enrollment form

Tell us a little about your French level, your goals, and your preferred format. It opens in a new tab.

We confirm your schedule

We'll follow up by email to confirm your cohort or arrange your one-on-one timing, and walk you through payment.

Join your first class

You'll get a link to your first live session, plus your first set of self-study materials.
