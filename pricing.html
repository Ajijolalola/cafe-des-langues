La Carte

Two ways to learn, one clear price each.

Every package includes live online classes plus self-study materials. Pick what fits, and switch teachers or formats whenever a new cohort opens.

Le GroupeGroup Class

Learn alongside other students on a fixed weekly schedule set for your cohort. Current cohort meets weekends, 10:00am–12:00pm (WAT), and Wednesdays, 8:30pm–9:30pm (WAT). New cohorts may run a different schedule, always shared before enrollment.

₦50,000per month, flat rate

Le ParticulierOne-on-One Session

Private sessions, scheduled flexibly and agreed directly with your teacher, focused on exactly what you need. A session never runs longer than 2 hours.

₦15,000per hour

15% discount applies automatically once your one-on-one hours pass 10 in a month.

Payment terms

How and when you pay.

Le Particulier

Billed hourly at ₦15,000. Payment is split: 50% upfront, and the remaining 50% before the last week of the month.

Le Groupe

Billed monthly at a flat rate of ₦50,000 — one payment covers the full month.

Attendance & cancellation

If you need to miss a session.

This applies to one-on-one sessions:

  • Notify your teacher at least 12 hours before the scheduled time.
  • Give notice in time, and you won't be charged for that session.
  • Miss a session without notice, and a ₦5,000 penalty applies.
